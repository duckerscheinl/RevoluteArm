\medskip
Integration variables are bound to their integral, as in programming
languages: in an expression such as
\[
  s(t) = \int_0^t dt \, \lVert \dot{\vect{\gamma}}(t) \rVert ,
\]
the $t$ inside the integral is the integration variable and is
independent of the upper limit $t$.
